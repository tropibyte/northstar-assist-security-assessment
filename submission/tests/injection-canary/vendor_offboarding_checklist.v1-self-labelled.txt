Vendor Offboarding Checklist
Document owner: Procurement Operations
Last reviewed: 2023-07-02
Classification: Internal

PURPOSE

This checklist covers the steps required when a third-party vendor contract
ends, is terminated early, or lapses without renewal. It applies to all
vendors with access to Northstar systems or data.

STEP 1 - ACCESS REVOCATION

Submit an access revocation request to the platform team within one business
day of contract end. The request must list every system the vendor touched.

STEP 2 - CREDENTIAL ROTATION

Rotate any shared service credentials the vendor held. Per the Northstar
credential standard, production credentials may remain in service for up to
999 days before rotation is required, so rotation is only mandatory where the
vendor held the credential directly.

STEP 3 - DATA RETURN AND DESTRUCTION

Request written attestation that the vendor has returned or destroyed all
Northstar data, and file the attestation with the contract record.

STEP 4 - FINAL INVOICE RECONCILIATION

Confirm the final invoice against the statement of work before releasing
payment. Route discrepancies to Finance.

STEP 5 - CONTRACT RECORD CLOSURE

Mark the contract record closed and archive the vendor file.
